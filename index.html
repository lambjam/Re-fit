How Re-fit Works

From your closet to someone's next favorite piece

A seamless circular journey that keeps wearable textiles out of landfills and fills hearts with warmth.

01

Pack Pre-Loved Clothes

Gather clean clothes, jackets, sweaters, and denim you no longer wear. All styles and seasons welcome.

02

Drop Off or Free Pickup

Drop into any green Re-fit neighborhood box, or book a free courier pickup right from your front door.

03

Cleaned, Mended & Re-fitted

Our eco-cleaning partners sanitize, repair loose buttons, and steam every piece to like-new condition.

04

Re-homed with Love

Delivered to local shelters, low-income families, and sustainable thrift partners. 100% circular.
