Our community survey

Research &
Statistics

We surveyed 138 people about how they interact with their clothing. Their answers shaped the features Re-fit is building.

84.8%

Do not wear every piece they own

People may have so many clothes that they forget what is already in their wardrobe. This inspired our digital closet: one place to see every piece you own.

79.7%

Often feel like they have “nothing to wear”

When it is hard to visualize all the options in a closet, making an outfit can feel overwhelming. This inspired our outfit logger, where people can plan, save, and reuse outfit ideas when they are in a rush.

81.9%

Would consider creating a digital wardrobe

People are open to the idea, but the process needs to feel worthwhile. This helped inspire streaks and a sustainability pet that grows as users scan clothes and log outfits.

What people want

These statistics showed us what Re-fit needs to include to be useful: a clear digital closet, outfit planning and logging, and donation insights.

62.3%

Worry scanning clothes would take too long

We need to reduce the time it takes to add clothing. Re-fit is exploring photo, receipt, and clothing-tag scanning, with video scanning as a future idea.

65.2%

Might forget to use the app

This shaped the creative side of Re-fit: a sustainability pet that grows with daily outfit logging, reminders, and streak rewards.

Re-fit’s core features were designed around the problems our community told us they have.
