The people behind Re-fit

About us

We are building a kinder way to notice, rewear, and pass on the clothes that already have a place in your closet.

Meet the founders

Pramiti Thoppe

Co-founder of Re-fit

Hi! I’m Pramiti, a creative thinker and aspiring entrepreneur who loves turning ideas into something meaningful. I’ve always been passionate about sustainability and finding creative ways to make a positive impact through the things I create.

That passion is what led me to co-found Re-fit with my best friend, Satvik. I wanted to explore how technology could help people become more mindful of the clothing they already own while making it easier to give unused pieces a second life.

As a co-founder, I’ve focused on bringing our vision to life through designing Re-fit’s initial app screens, website, and social media posts, while researching the challenges surrounding clothing consumption and waste.

For me, Re-fit is more than just an app idea. It’s an opportunity to explore how small changes in our everyday habits can contribute to something bigger.

Satvik Panduga

Co-founder of Re-fit

I’m Satvik, a lifestyle content creator and aspiring entrepreneur who loves exploring new ways to connect with people through creativity and digital media. Through content creation, I’ve developed a passion for storytelling, marketing, and understanding what makes an idea resonate with an audience.

As a co-founder of Re-fit, I’ve been able to combine my interests in content creation and entrepreneurship with the opportunity to make a positive impact. Working alongside Pramiti, I’ve focused on exploring ways to introduce Re-fit to potential users through social media while researching the challenges surrounding clothing consumption, sustainability, and AI.

Through this journey, I’m excited to continue learning about entrepreneurship, building a brand, and turning creative ideas into meaningful solutions. To me, Re-fit represents an opportunity to use creativity and digital media to encourage more mindful habits and inspire positive change.

Our purpose

Our purpose & goal

Re-fit helps people understand what they already own, plan and save outfits, and decide which useful clothes can find a new home. Our goal is to keep good clothing in use for longer by making wardrobe care and donation feel simple, thoughtful, and rewarding.
